SETUP (after creating the project with: flutter create qr_generator)

1. Replace pubspec.yaml and the lib/ folder with the ones in this project.
2. Run: flutter pub get

ANDROID - android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
  Inside <manifest>, above <application>, add ONE line so saving works on
  Android 9 and older (Android 10+ needs no permission for gallery saves):

  <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE"
      android:maxSdkVersion="28" />

ANDROID - minSdk
  gal requires minSdkVersion 24 or higher. In android/app/build.gradle(.kts)
  set minSdk = 24 (or flutter.minSdkVersion if it is already 24+).

IOS - ios/Runner/Info.plist
  Add these keys inside the top <dict>, otherwise saving will crash on iOS:

  <key>NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription</key>
  <string>Save generated QR codes to your photo library.</string>
  <key>NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription</key>
  <string>Save generated QR codes to your photo library.</string>
